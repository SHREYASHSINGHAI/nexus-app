Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your active NEXUS subscription at any time. When you cancel, your premium features and weekly workflow session limits will remain active until the end of your current monthly billing period. After this date, your account will automatically downgrade to the Free tier (3 workflows/week).

2. 7-Day Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with NEXUS. We offer a full refund on your initial subscription purchase under the following conditions:

  • The refund request is submitted within 7 days of your initial payment date.
  • You have consumed less than 20% of your purchased weekly workflow quota during this period.
  • Technical issues prevented you from accessing the service and our support team was unable to resolve them.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please send an email to support@nexus.ai with the subject line Refund Request - [Your Registered Email]. Please include:

  • Your registered account email address
  • The Razorpay Payment ID or Order ID from your receipt
  • A brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Once your refund request is received and verified, we will initiate the refund via Razorpay. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, or Digital Wallet) within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's processing cycles.

5. Inquiries & Disputes

For any billing inquiries, payment discrepancies, or transaction support, please reach out directly to billing@nexus.ai.
